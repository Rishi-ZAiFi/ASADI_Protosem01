( What the algorithm rewards )

Six signals we write for.

a

Saves & DM shares

Widely reported as the strongest reach signals. Captions that offer something worth revisiting or sending to a friend win.

b

The first 125 characters

Everything before "more" is your headline. Lead with tension, a number or a claim, not a description.

c

Caption SEO

Instagram search reads caption text. Plain keywords like "beginner pilates at home" beat "vibes".

d

One clear CTA

One specific ask at the end. A direct question beats "thoughts?" and two CTAs split the action.

e

Few, relevant hashtags

A handful of targeted tags, aligned with your keywords, over a wall of generic ones.

f

Hook + watch time

For Reels the first 3 seconds decide it. Captions should extend the video, not repeat it.
