Topic: How to use LangGraph for AI agents

Hook: Stop building static AI agents and start creating dynamic workflows.

Caption:
Stop building static AI agents and start creating dynamic workflows.

Most LLM applications struggle with complex reasoning because they lack a loop. LangGraph changes the game by allowing your agents to maintain state across multiple turns. It treats your agent as a graph of nodes and edges, making it infinitely easier to manage long-running processes and human-in-the-loop interactions. 🚀 By structuring your logic this way, you gain full control over the execution flow rather than relying on a single prompt. It is the missing piece for production-grade AI systems. 💡
